In the Persian Gulf, the Iranian navy says a tanker struck a mine in the Strait of Hormuz today and caught fire. That account has not been independently confirmed.
Earlier, the British maritime agency UKMTO reported a ship burning in the strait, with the status of its crew unknown. Iran's Revolutionary Guard had claimed that three tankers hit mines. That claim also has not been verified.
This evening, Iran's Fars news agency reported explosions near Sirik and Qeshm Island. The Revolutionary Guard says it intercepted a, quote, "hostile," end quote, fighter jet over Qeshm. There is no independent confirmation.
US Central Command denied an Iranian claim that Iran had captured an American drone.
On diplomacy, Iran's Tasnim news agency says Tehran received a letter from Vice President Vance and has not yet replied. President Trump says the letter was about medical supplies. Iran's parliament speaker, Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, says Iran's seven conditions are the only path forward.
US Ambassador Tom Barrack says the two sides are very close to agreeing on a process to de-escalate. He added that more US military action is still possible.
Israel's state broadcaster reports that 10 US military transport aircraft landed at the Ovda and Nevatim air bases in the past 24 hours. It says Israel is preparing for a possible US attack on Iran. US media report Washington warned Gulf allies of possible strikes after next week's midterms. That has not been officially confirmed.
In Washington, Secretary Pete Hegseth fired the Pentagon's top military planner, Lieutenant General Brett Sylvia. CBS News and Reuters report he is accused of leaking classified information. President Trump says he is, quote, "looking for leakers," end quote.
South Korea is weighing a deployment to the Strait of Hormuz, including aircraft, Reuters reports.
A Reuters investigation found an Iranian network imported at least $2B in hardware past the US blockade since January 2025. Reuters also reports an Iranian military agency imported rocket propellants, explosives and trucks that can serve as missile launchers.
Europe will release 42M barrels of oil from its reserves over three months, Bloomberg reports.
In Saudi Arabia, the Saudi-led coalition says it intercepted two Houthi ballistic missiles fired toward Riyadh. The Saudi Press Agency reports no damage.
