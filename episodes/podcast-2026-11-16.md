Russia launched a large ballistic missile attack across Ukraine this evening.
Kyiv Mayor Vitali Klitschko says preliminary reports indicate impacts on the North and South bridges over the Dnipro River in the capital.
Earlier today, President Zelensky warned of imminent Russian attacks and urged people to heed air-raid alerts.
The strikes followed what AFP described as a record Ukrainian drone attack toward Moscow overnight.
AFP reports the attack also hit the Crimean Bridge. The Kyiv Independent reports the strike damaged an arch over the bridge's navigable span, and traffic was restricted.
Late this evening, the Kyiv Independent reported that Ukrainian forces struck the Crimean Bridge again. That report has not been independently confirmed. Claims online that the bridge collapsed are also unconfirmed.
Ukraine also confirmed a strike on an oil depot in Rostov-on-Don.
Officials in Ukraine say Russian attacks killed 6 people and injured 45 in the past day.
Nuclear regulators from 38 countries warned of a rising risk of a nuclear accident in Ukraine, as attacks on the power grid continue.
The European Union adopted new sanctions on Russia. They target the owner of the retailer Wildberries, 50 banks, oil trading and the so-called shadow fleet of tankers. The E.U. also disbursed 1.7 billion euros in military aid.

Iran's navy says a fourth tanker caught fire today after striking a sea mine near the Strait of Hormuz.
Iran's Revolutionary Guard says the tankers hit were on a route that Tehran calls unauthorized.
The Guard also says it seized an American MQ-170 drone near Qeshm Island. The United States has not confirmed that claim.
A US official says the aircraft carrier Theodore Roosevelt has arrived in the Middle East. Al Jazeera reports that puts 3 American carriers and more than 200 aircraft in the region.
Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi is in Muscat, Oman, for talks on the strait.
British Prime Minister Andy Burnham says his government is supporting mediators seeking an agreement to end the war, reopen the strait and prevent Iran from developing a nuclear weapon.
Iran warns of severe gas shortages this winter. It cites the American naval blockade and the war.
CNBC reports US crude fell to $87 a barrel as Middle East exports recover.
