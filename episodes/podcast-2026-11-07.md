## World: Poland

Sources: The Guardian https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/nov/07/krakow-poland-shooting-market-square-deaths-gunman ; BBC https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cmd7q15drwgeo ; AP https://apnews.com/article/poland-shooting-krakow-market-dead-076b972f107df14a1f71e12a74d4d8e5 ; CNA (AFP) https://www.channelnewsasia.com/world/gunman-fascinated-far-right-kills-six-in-krakow-poland-6533446
In Poland, a gunman opened fire in the Main Market Square of Kraków this afternoon.
Police say a man drove a car into the square shortly before 3 P.M. local time and fired at random people from the vehicle.
Six people were killed, four women and two men. Police say one of the dead is a British national. Sixteen people were taken to hospital, including two police officers.
The BBC reports officers shot the suspect within about three minutes. He is alive and under arrest. One wounded officer is described by a government minister as in critical condition.
Police describe the suspect as a 25-year-old Polish citizen. He has not been publicly named.
Prime Minister Donald Tusk says three more bodies, two women and a man, were found at the suspect’s home. Tusk says there is much to suggest they were members of his immediate family.
Interior Minister Marcin Kierwiński says the suspect held, quote, "very radical" and "ultra-nationalistic" views, end quote. Police say he acted alone. A motive has not been formally established.
Agence France-Presse calls it Poland’s deadliest shooting in decades. All figures are preliminary.

## World: Ukraine

In Ukraine, Russia struck Kyiv and the surrounding region overnight.
President Zelensky says Russia launched 36 ballistic missiles and 172 drones. Ukraine’s Air Force says it intercepted or suppressed four of the missiles and 149 of the drones.
Ukrainian officials say one person was killed and at least eight were wounded. The Associated Press reports at least nine people were wounded across Ukraine on Saturday. The figures are preliminary.
Mayor Vitali Klitschko says missiles hit the Northern Bridge, which is closed in both directions. He describes the city’s power, heat and water supply as, quote, "extremely difficult," end quote.
Russia’s Defense Ministry says it targeted the bridge and a plant it says makes Flamingo cruise missiles. It says the bridge was used to move military equipment.
Zelensky says Russia is trying to push the country into a blackout before talks.
Those talks are set for Tuesday in Abu Dhabi. The Kremlin says presidential aide Vladimir Medinsky will lead Russia’s delegation, with senior diplomats and military intelligence officials. Ukraine’s team will be led by Rustem Umerov.
