In Ukraine, a Russian drone attack collapsed part of a 5-story apartment building in Dymer, in the Kyiv region, President Zelensky says.
The Kyiv Independent reports the death toll has risen to 6. Earlier this evening, Mr. Zelensky said there was still no information on 10 people.
Rescuers pulled a 7-year-old boy from the rubble after a search that lasted all day, the BBC reports.
Mr. Zelensky said Russia fired 165 missiles at Ukraine in the past week. He urged the United States to approve deliveries of Patriot air defense systems.
In Poland, prosecutors say 3 Russian missiles flew into Polish airspace on Friday. They say debris from K.H. one-oh-one cruise missiles was found at 3 sites in the east of the country.
In Libya, an explosion struck a Russian ship at a naval base in Benghazi. Libya's eastern government says it suspects a drone, Reuters reports.
Al Jazeera reports a second attack later targeted a Maltese-flagged commercial vessel off Benghazi. Libyan forces reportedly intercepted more than 20 naval drones.
The Associated Press, citing unnamed sources, reports the drones were Ukrainian sea drones launched from a base run by Ukrainian forces in western Libya. That has not been independently confirmed.
The U.N. mission in Libya expressed serious concern and called for an independent investigation.
And off Finland, a Russian cargo ship bound for Kaliningrad was still burning today after a suspected drone attack, Reuters reports.
