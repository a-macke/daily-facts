Saudi Aramco has evacuated its headquarters in Dhahran, in Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province.
Reuters, citing sources, and Bloomberg reported the evacuation late this morning. No reason was given publicly.
The move follows Wednesday night's attack on King Fahd International Airport in Dammam.
The Saudi civil aviation authority says one person was killed and eight were injured there.
Al Jazeera reports two people were killed, including an Indian citizen. India has denounced the attack.
Bloomberg reported overnight that three people were killed in two attacks, one on an Aramco oil field and one on an airport. That account comes from a single source.
Saudi authorities have not said who carried out the attacks.
This afternoon, the Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted a Houthi missile fired toward the city of Najran.
United Nations Secretary-General António Guterres called the attacks unacceptable.
Egypt is calling for talks on Yemen, as its parliament weighs military support for Saudi Arabia. A vote is expected Monday.
The State Department says its departure order for non-emergency staff and families applies to Saudi Arabia only.
And the World Health Organization says 1,574 people have been killed and 6,939 injured in Yemen since August.
