In Saudi Arabia, King Fahd International Airport in Dammam was attacked this evening.
The Saudi civil aviation authority says one resident was killed and eight were injured. It reports material damage.
Saudi authorities have not said who carried out the attack, or with what weapon.
The Saudi-led coalition says it intercepted several drones over the Eastern Province.
Separately, the Saudi energy ministry says two people were killed by falling debris near the Shaybah oil field, after a drone was intercepted. These figures are preliminary.
Earlier today, the State Department ordered non-emergency staff and their families to leave Saudi Arabia.
An AFP diplomatic source says families of embassy staff in the United Arab Emirates, Jordan, Qatar and Bahrain were also ordered out.
US officials told Reuters the move anticipates renewed fighting, especially with the Houthis in Yemen.
President Sisi of Egypt says any Egyptian military support would defend Saudi territory. He ruled out an attack on Yemen.
France and Britain are moving military assets to Saudi Arabia. Both say the deployments are defensive.
The Houthis deny sending drones toward Medina, Reuters reports.
In Yemen's Shabwa province, a mass funeral was held for 75 recruits killed in a Houthi missile strike.

Turning to Iran. Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says the Strait of Hormuz will stay closed until Washington accepts Iran's conditions.
Bloomberg reports those conditions include lifting the US naval blockade.
President Pezeshkian says there will be no dialogue while the United States blockades and threatens Iran.
Secretary of State Rubio has called a deal, quote, "extremely difficult," end quote.
Prime Minister Netanyahu said Iran's supreme leader, Mojtaba Khamenei, must be removed.
A Revolutionary Guards commander said Iran would, quote, "invade," end quote, any attacker.
Iranian media reported explosions in the coastal town of Sirik. The source has not been identified.
In Gaza, the health ministry there says about 74,500 people have been killed and about 175,600 wounded since the war began.
And Israeli media report Israeli forces detained former New York City comptroller Brad Lander during the olive harvest in the West Bank.
