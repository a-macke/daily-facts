Earlier, Tehran called the American blockade of its ports an act of military aggression.
Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei said Iran would use all available means to counter it.

In Yemen, Houthi-run media say at least 10 people were killed and more than 20 wounded in an attack on a telecommunications building in Hodeidah. They did not say who carried it out.

## Second Amendment cases

In Second Amendment cases, the Supreme Court acted today in the challenge to bans on semiautomatic rifles.
The case, Viramontes versus Cook County, is consolidated with a Connecticut case, Grant versus Higgins.
The court dismissed lead petitioner Cutberto Viramontes, at his own request. It did not dismiss the case itself.
Cook County has argued the Illinois case is now moot. The remaining petitioners say it is not. The court has not ruled on that question.
Oral argument remains set for December 2nd. The Justice Department supports the challengers. Cook County and Connecticut defend their laws.
