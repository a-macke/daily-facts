The Kyiv Independent reports that a section of the Crimean Bridge collapsed early today, after a strike by Ukrainian Flamingo missiles.
AFP reports a section of the bridge is in the water.
The bridge links Russia to occupied Crimea.
Ukrainian officials say Russian attacks killed 14 people and injured 78 in the past day. That includes Monday night's missile strikes on bridges in Kyiv.
President Zelensky says talks with the United States and European partners could be held in Berlin as early as Thursday, November 19th.

A draft European Union plan would provide 45 billion euros more in loans to Ukraine in 2027, the Kyiv Independent reports.

The United States struck a missile launch site of Iran's Revolutionary Guard on Qeshm Island today.
A U.S. military official told Al Jazeera the strike was an act of self-defense, in response to Iranian aggression against American forces.
Iranian media say the strikes caused no damage to infrastructure.
Overnight, the Revolutionary Guard claimed a strike on an American radar at Isa Air Base in Bahrain. Bahrain says the attack was thwarted.
This evening, Bahrain's Interior Ministry issued a potential-threat alert. It urged residents to avoid main roads. About an hour later, it issued an all-clear. No attack or damage has been confirmed.
Bloomberg reports Iran has threatened tit-for-tat attacks on civilian infrastructure near American and Israeli bases.
Accounts differ on a CNN report, citing intelligence, that Iran may be planning a radiological or chemical attack. That report has not been independently confirmed.
President Trump said, quote, we take it very seriously, end quote. He warned of unprecedented consequences.
Al Jazeera reports the White House says there is no credible threat, and that no evidence has been presented.
Iran denies the report. Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei called it baseless. Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi also rejected it.
In Washington, Acting Navy Secretary Hung Cao told senators the war with Iran is straining American maritime forces. Senators raised concerns about deployments and weapons stockpiles.
And Yemen's Houthis say they launched drones toward Saudi Arabia's East-West oil pipeline.
