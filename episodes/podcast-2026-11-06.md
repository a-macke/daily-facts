In Washington, President Trump announced that Stephen Miller will replace Susie Wiles as White House chief of staff.
The change takes effect Monday.
Miller has been deputy chief of staff for policy. Wiles submitted her resignation Thursday night, according to NBC News.
Mr. Trump said Wiles will move to his political operation. He thanked her for her service.
The Associated Press reports the move is the most significant White House staff change since Tuesday’s midterm elections.
On the economy, the Labor Department says U.S. employers added 165,000 jobs in October.
The Associated Press reports that was well above what economists expected. The unemployment rate fell to 4.1%.
CNBC reports average hourly earnings rose 0.4% for the month, and 3.1% over the year. Prior months were revised higher.
On Wall Street, stocks partly recovered from Thursday’s selloff. CNBC reports the Nasdaq rose 1.09%. The S&P 500 gained 0.3%. The Dow fell 237 points.
At the Justice Department, CBS News reports John Eisenberg, the assistant attorney general for national security, resigned Friday. Jason Reding Quiñones, the U.S. attorney in Miami, also resigned.
Agence France-Presse reports the United States has recorded 5,182 measles cases so far this year, the highest total in 35 years.

## World: Ukraine
<!-- summary: Russian ballistic missiles hit Kyiv overnight; Klitschko reports residential and kindergarten damage; at least two wounded; Zaporizhzhia aid-center toll rises to 53. -->
Sources: Kyiv Independent https://kyivindependent.com/russian-ballistic-missiles-slam-kyiv-after-zelensky-warns-of-mass-attack/ ; Kyiv Post https://www.kyivpost.com/post/89404 ; CBC (Zaporizhzhia) https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/ukraine-zaporizhzhia-attack-russia-melania-trump-9.7411754
Overnight, Russian ballistic missiles struck Kyiv in several waves.
The Kyiv Independent reports Ukraine’s Air Force detected at least six ballistic missiles heading toward the capital shortly before 11 P.M. local time. Further waves followed after midnight.
Mayor Vitali Klitschko said Russia was attacking infrastructure with ballistic missiles. He later reported damage to a residential building and a kindergarten in the Solomianskyi district.
The Kyiv City Military Administration said at least two people in the city were wounded. A third person was injured in the Brovary district. No deaths in Kyiv city have been confirmed. The figures are preliminary.
Earlier Friday, President Zelensky warned of Russian preparations for new strikes on energy facilities, bridges and civilian infrastructure.
Separately, the death toll from a Russian strike on an aid center in Zaporizhzhia rose to 53. Search crews continued into a fifth day. Dozens remain missing. Those numbers are preliminary.

In the Gulf, Iranian media reported explosions on Qeshm Island this evening.
Tasnim, linked to Iran’s Revolutionary Guard, said initial reports indicated two missiles struck the island. Iran’s official news agency cited witnesses who said two projectiles hit the Suza area after aircraft were heard overhead.
Iranian state television later said no casualties were confirmed. A U.S. Central Command official told Al Jazeera the situation near Qeshm was, quote, "relatively calm," end quote. The origin of the reported projectiles has not been independently confirmed.
Reuters reports one seafarer was killed in a suspected missile strike Thursday on the oil products tanker MT Velos Sapphire. The ship’s manager confirmed the death Friday.
U.S. embassies in Jerusalem, Iraq, Bahrain and elsewhere issued security alerts, citing Iranian efforts to target U.S. officials and risks from Houthi attacks.
President Trump told reporters that talks with Iran are, quote, "going very well," end quote. Qatar denied reports that mediation had failed.

## World: Paraguay
<!-- summary: Senate removes Peña 44–0 over Ueno Bank; Alliana sworn in; Peña under house arrest. -->
Sources: AP https://apnews.com/article/paraguay-santiago-pena-bank-failure-ueno-impeachment-trial-resign-crisis-e84f5ada84e8c747329ec82fabb46e4d ; Al Jazeera https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/7/paraguays-santiago-pena-under-arrest-new-president-pedro-alliana-sworn-in
In Paraguay, the Senate removed President Santiago Peña from office Friday.
The Associated Press reports all 44 senators present voted to remove him for misconduct tied to the collapse of Ueno Bank. Millions of customers lost access to savings after regulators intervened earlier this week.
Vice President Pedro Alliana was sworn in as president. Prosecutors then placed Peña under house arrest on suspicion of breach of trust. Peña has denied wrongdoing.

Here at home, cleanup continued after Thursday’s jet-fuel spill in Upper Merion.
WHYY reports a PECO contractor struck a Sunoco pipeline on River Road near Bridgeport. Officials say the spill was contained to an auto-auction property and an adjacent quarry, with no expected threat to drinking water. Roads have reopened.
Estimates of the volume still differ. Some local reports cited about 60,000 gallons. Earlier comments to the Inquirer cited a much larger Sunoco estimate. Officials have not settled on a final number.
In Philadelphia, Caprice Williams, 36, was held without bail after a Friday arraignment on murder and related charges in the death of 10-year-old Malayshia Alford.
In Mount Holly, New Jersey, three elderly people were found dead Friday in a home on Winding Way. A postal worker saw a note telling someone to call 911. The Burlington County prosecutor said there is no danger to the public. Autopsies are planned this weekend.
In Chester County, MyChesCo reports Tower Health posted its first audited operating profit since 2017, while Phoenixville Hospital lost $10M.
East Whiteland voters expanded the board of supervisors from three seats to five after a fight over a data center. The vote does not reverse an existing approval.
A federal judge ruled Philadelphia does not have to honor ICE jail detainers.
And Pennsylvania said goodbye to two public figures. Former state Chief Justice Ronald D. Castille has died. Julian A. Brodsky, a Comcast co-founder, died Thursday at 93.
