## World: Iran, Bahrain and the Gulf

Iran's Revolutionary Guard says it targeted an American air defense radar at Riffa Air Base in Bahrain overnight, using 6 drones.
The Guard calls the strike retaliation for an attack on a telecommunications tower in Sirik, in southern Iran.
The Bahrain Defence Force says its air defenses destroyed several Iranian aerial attacks. Sirens sounded, and residents were told to take shelter.
No casualties or damage have been reported.
A senior American official told Al Jazeera the attack failed and caused no damage. US Central Command has not publicly confirmed that account.
Bahrain's Foreign Ministry denounced what it called, quote, "the resumption of Iran's aggression," end quote.
Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Jordan and the Arab League condemned the attack.
Iran, for its part, says the path to ending the conflict remains open, Bloomberg reports. Al Jazeera reports Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi will meet his Omani counterpart this week.

Ukraine says Russia launched 132 drones overnight, and that 106 were shot down.
Ukrainian officials say 4 people were killed and 69 injured across the country in the past day. About 41,000 families in the Odesa region lost power, the Kyiv Independent reports.
Ukraine's General Staff says its drones struck the Akhtubinsk airfield, a Russian military flight test center, along with targets in Astrakhan and Oryol.
President Zelenskyy says Ukrainian drone attacks have, quote, "neutralized," end quote, 55% of Russia's oil refining capacity. That figure has not been independently confirmed.
On diplomacy, Mr. Zelenskyy says American negotiators floated the idea of Ukraine ceding the Donbas in exchange for NATO membership. AFP reports meetings are being prepared to define a European position.
In Estonia, authorities say Russian military intelligence ordered a failed arson attack at a drone maker, Threod Systems. Eight people have been arrested. Estonia says it is closing its border crossings with Russia.

Pressure on Nvidia grew today. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent called for a, quote, "dramatic," end quote, leadership shakeup at the company.
