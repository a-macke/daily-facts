Bahrain's Interior Ministry says an Iranian attack today hit a civilian industrial facility near the capital, Manama.
The ministry says the strike caused a fire and material damage, but no injuries. It says air defenses intercepted other attacks.
Iran's Revolutionary Guard says it struck 2 U.S. Patriot radar sites at bases in Bahrain with missiles and drones. It cited what it called U.S. drone violations of Iranian airspace.
There has been no U.S. confirmation of that claim.
Bahrain condemned what it called unlawful attacks and appealed to the U.N. Security Council.
At sea, the British maritime agency U.K.M.T.O. reported 2 incidents involving tankers near Oman today. Iran says a vessel struck a mine east of the Strait of Hormuz.
The United States denied an Iranian claim that a missile damaged an American F-16 over the Gulf, Reuters reports.
Iran says Qatari mediators are in Tehran. Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi said the talks are not yet formal negotiations. He called the U.S. naval blockade, quote, an act of war, end quote.
Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said reopening the strait would, quote, not be difficult, end quote, under what he called reasonable conditions.
President Trump said Iran's terms are unacceptable. He said he plans a, quote, permanent, end quote, deal that would prevent Iran from obtaining a nuclear weapon.
In Saudi Arabia, the Saudi-led coalition says it intercepted 3 Houthi ballistic missiles aimed at Riyadh on Friday night. No damage or casualties were reported.
Turkey's Anadolu agency reports more Houthi missiles were intercepted today. Claims of new damage in Riyadh have not been confirmed.

A Russian cargo ship caught fire this morning in the eastern Baltic after what Finland describes as a drone attack, Reuters and AFP report.
The ship drifted into Finnish waters. Russia says the vessel was struck by drones.
No one has claimed the attack, and officials have not identified who carried it out.
Later, the Kyiv Independent reported explosions near Russia's Baltic Fleet base at Baltiysk, in the Kaliningrad region, during drone alerts.
Moldova and Finland both reported Russian airspace violations today. That came a day after suspected Russian missiles entered Polish airspace.
In Ukraine, authorities say a Russian strike on Kryvyi Rih was followed by a second strike. They say it killed a child and a young rescuer responding to the first.
President Zelensky said Ukraine has not yet seen written commitments on NATO-style security guarantees. He said Ukraine remains determined to join NATO.
And just after 2 a.m. local time, explosions were heard in Kyiv as Ukraine's Air Force warned of approaching Russian drones.

The Consumer Product Safety Commission says about 229,000 Johnson Level digital angle locators, calipers and calculators are recalled, along with CLC lighted portfolios.
The agency says the products violate the federal standard for button cell batteries, posing a risk of serious injury or death if a battery is swallowed.
The products were sold at Lowe's, Amazon and other retailers from February 2023 through November 2026. Owners can get a refund by sending a photo of the disposed product.
On the West Coast, a port strike has been averted. The Pacific Maritime Association and the union local representing about 250 watchmen at Los Angeles and Long Beach reached a tentative deal.
