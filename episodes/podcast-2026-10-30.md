In the Strait of Hormuz, a tanker was hit by limpet mines today, Reuters reports, citing security sources. Bloomberg reports fighters in speedboats attached the explosives, damaging the ship's engine. Bloomberg says it is the first attack of its kind in this conflict.
Oman's navy says it evacuated 25 crew members off the Musandam peninsula.
Iran's Revolutionary Guard navy says three tankers are on fire. That claim has not been confirmed. The Guard also says it stopped two oil tankers in the southern part of the strait.
The head of US Central Command, Admiral Brad Cooper, says the main shipping routes are clear of mines.
On diplomacy, Qatari mediators returned to Tehran and coordinated with US officials, Reuters reports. Pakistan's military says it is cautiously optimistic about its own mediation, but warns of spoilers.
President Trump says Iran wants to negotiate, and that the two sides are in talks. Earlier, he said the war should, quote, "get wrapped up soon," end quote.
Democratic Senator Chris Murphy says he was briefed on a plan to restart the war after the midterm elections. That is his description. The administration has not confirmed it.
In Tehran, Al Jazeera reports Iran's parliament has more than 10 proposals to leave the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty. Fars news agency quotes an Iranian official as saying an updated military plan is final, and that any response to a new attack would be, quote, "comprehensive," end quote.
In Saudi Arabia, the United States ordered non-emergency American staff to leave the country. The Saudi-led coalition says it destroyed a Houthi drone over the Medina region. This morning it said it intercepted a drone headed toward the Mecca region. The Houthis deny launching that drone.
In Yemen, a police official told AFP the Houthis fired two missiles at the presidential palace in Aden.
And in the West Bank, Al Jazeera reports an Israeli siege in Hebron has displaced at least 4,969 people.

The United States approved the sale of $2.1B in Patriot air defense equipment to Ukraine, Reuters and Bloomberg report.
In the occupied town of Oleshky, some residents were evacuated on the fourth day of a local ceasefire. Reuters says it is the first joint relief operation of the war. CBS News reports the evacuees are traveling through Russia and Belarus, and are expected back in Ukraine on Saturday. Russia says the humanitarian mission is complete.
Ukraine's military says Russia suffered a record 49,040 casualties in October. That figure has not been independently verified.

In Chester County, the government headquarters in West Chester was locked down this morning after a worker found a powdery substance in a ballot envelope. The lockdown lasted from about 10:15 to 11:30.
Accounts differ on one point. The Daily Local quotes Commissioner Josh Maxwell as saying an employee opened the ballot. 6abc reports investigators said the substance was detected from outside the envelope, and that it was not opened.
Officials say no one was hurt, and there was no sign of a chemical or biological hazard. The envelope has been sent to the FBI, which is trying to identify the substance. The District Attorney's office is investigating. It is not yet known whether the ballot was mailed or dropped off.
Elsewhere in the county, Downingtown is weighing limits on e-bikes and a new fire code. An affiliate of the Clemens family bought a Downingtown retail center for $22M. East Reeceville Road is closing, with a detour expected to last three weeks. And Kennett schools have added real-time bus tracking.
For commuters, power problems delayed most SEPTA Regional Rail lines by up to 30 minutes this evening. Some trains on the Paoli Thorndale line were canceled.
Across the region, AP reports Pennsylvania's Amish Country is the center of the worst US measles outbreak in 35 years.
In Philadelphia, Comcast is laying off more than 600 local employees, the Inquirer reports. The school board approved next steps in a plan to close 17 schools, amid protests. The Inquirer reports Philadelphia is again the poorest large city in the country. In Lower Merion, a data center has been proposed in Bala Cynwyd.
For the weather, forecasters expect a cooler, sunny Halloween. And clocks fall back one hour at 2 AM Sunday.
