Iran's navy and Revolutionary Guard say fires broke out today on a total of 3 oil tankers that struck mines in the Strait of Hormuz, AFP reports.
The Guard says two of the tankers were trying to transit, quote, "secretly," end quote, with their tracking systems turned off.
These are Iranian accounts. They have not been independently confirmed.
The United Nations says at least 25 seafarers have been killed in and around the strait since the US-Israeli war on Iran began in February.
Reuters, citing sources, reports President Trump has told advisers he may hold off on attacks on Iran unless there are new provocations.
Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei says the United States is openly threatening to starve Iranians through an economic blockade. He was responding to a statement by Secretary of State Marco Rubio.
In Saudi Arabia, the US Embassy in Riyadh urged Americans in the Middle East to be more vigilant, citing an increased threat of attacks by the Houthis. The Saudi-led coalition says it intercepted 2 Houthi drones launched toward Riyadh and the south of the country.
In Gaza, the Israeli military says it struck a senior commander in the military wing of Hamas.
The Arab League condemned an Israeli attack on Nasser Hospital. Al Jazeera reports that attack killed at least 6 Palestinians. And the European Union is urging Israel to reopen all border crossings into Gaza after severe flooding there this week.

Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy says air traffic operations have fully resumed after Friday's nationwide ground stop.
The cause is still being sorted out. The FAA says the, quote, "root cause," end quote, of the failure is unknown.
