In Mississippi, Democrat Scott Colom has defeated Republican Senator Cindy Hyde-Smith, the Associated Press projects. The result came after days of counting.
Mr. Colom will be Mississippi's first Black United States senator since Reconstruction. Al Jazeera reports it is the first Democratic Senate win in the state since 1982.
At Arlington National Cemetery, President Trump marked Veterans Day. He said it was time for, quote, "complete victory," end quote, against Iran. He also praised Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth.
Reuters reports video appears to show an Army sentinel at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier rebuking Mr. Hegseth's delegation, after laughter was heard on the steps.
Russia's ambassador to the United States has died, the Russian foreign ministry says. No cause of death or other details have been released.
The Agriculture Department has proposed a SNAP rule that would remove about 6.3 million people from food aid. An estimated 1.8 million children would lose eligibility for free school lunch.
The Trump administration formally said it plans to sell public lands to reduce the federal estate, Bloomberg reports.
Reuters reports the chair of the Federal Reserve disclosed his wife's transactions in pet-care investments.
In Brooklyn, a building on Flatbush Avenue partly collapsed into a neighboring construction site. Officials say at least one person died and several were injured. A husband told WABC that his pregnant wife was pulled alive from the rubble. Rescue work continues tonight.
